BindCraft Documentation
=======================

**BindCraft** is a pipeline for *de novo* protein binder design, combining AlphaFold2,
ProteinMPNN, and PyRosetta to design and score protein binders against a target of interest.

This documentation is aimed at users of all computational backgrounds — from wet-lab
biologists submitting their first job to experienced researchers who want to customise
the pipeline.
